# Methodology

For the source-by-source acquisition history, scraping failure modes, review
linkage rules, and the distinction between reported and inferred fields, see
[`data-acquisition-walkthrough.md`](data-acquisition-walkthrough.md).

## Challenge identity and outcome

An ABS challenge is an MLB live-feed review whose `reviewType` is `MJ`.
Pitch-event reviews are linked directly. A review attached only to a plate
appearance is linked to that plate appearance's terminal called pitch and is
flagged `plate_appearance_terminal`.

`reviewDetails.isOverturned = true` is an overturned call and explicit
`false` is an upheld call. Missing is never converted to `false`: unresolved
records stop inventory reconstruction and are quarantined before publication.
Success rate is `overturned / (overturned + upheld)`.

The live feed displays the final call after an overturn. Therefore the original
call equals the final call for an upheld review and its opposite for an
overturned review. Savant's `original_isStrike_ump` independently validates
this reconstruction.

## Geometry

For pitch center `(x, z)`, zone top `t`, zone bottom `b`, horizontal half-width
`17/24` feet, and ball radius `1.45` inches, define

```text
qx = abs(x) - 17/24
qz = max(b - z, z - t)
d_center = sqrt(max(qx,0)^2 + max(qz,0)^2) + min(max(qx,qz),0)
d_edge_inches = 12*d_center - 1.45
```

The call is an ABS strike when `d_edge_inches <= 0`. Statcast plate coordinates
and official top/bottom rounded to three decimal places reproduce Savant's
`edge_dist_calc` to floating-point tolerance, including zone corners.

## Opportunity and inventory

A correctable opportunity is an adverse original call that geometry says is
wrong. Called strikes belong to the batting team's opportunity set; called
balls belong to the fielding team's. Unchallenged correctable calls are split
by reconstructed inventory into `missed_available` and `out_of_challenges`.

Each team starts with two challenges. An overturn retains inventory, an upheld
call consumes one, and a team at zero replenishes to one when entering each
extra inning. The expected inventory curves use leave-one-game-out state
averages and a backward recursion under observed, cross-fitted league challenge
behavior. The 2-to-1 and 1-to-0 values are differences in reachable future
correct opportunities, RE, and WPA—not a universal fixed penalty.

The live feed's final `remaining` field reflects the two-challenge regulation
bucket and does not expose an unused extra-inning grant. It is reconciled in
regulation games only; extra-inning inventory is validated event by event so
that no challenge is allowed at zero after applying each inning's grant.

## Interpretation

Observed totals are descriptive census quantities. Geometry on unchallenged
pitches is inferred rather than an official ruling. Realized value at zero is
not a claim that the club would certainly have challenged. Team adjustments
control for observed opportunity mix and are not causal skill estimates.

## Historical model provenance

Savant's CSV endpoint truncates broad searches at 25,000 rows. The historical
model layer therefore reads a commit-pinned Parquet mirror generated by
`pybaseball` from Statcast for 2023–2024 and one-day official Savant exports for
2025. It records SHA-256 checksums and requires at least 99.5% coverage of
scheduled 2023–2025 regular-season games before fitting.
Official MLB live feeds and Savant ABS detail remain canonical for the 2026
challenge ledger and all ruling/geometry validation.
